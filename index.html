The problem

Isolation is a public health crisis hiding in plain sight.

Aging populations are growing faster than our systems can support them. Existing solutions are either expensive clinical platforms or fragmented volunteer programs with no real technology backbone. There's a huge gap for something warm, simple, and community-driven.

How it works

One platform, three communities

TheCompanionBridge serves seniors, their families, and local volunteers — each with their own tailored experience.

👴
For seniors

Stay connected with ease

Designed for large tablets with big buttons, high contrast, and simple navigation — built for the people who need it most.

  • One-tap daily check-in with mood
  • Request a companion or errand helper
  • Browse local events and activities
  • SOS alert button for emergencies
  • Simple video calls with family
👨‍👩‍👧
For families

Peace of mind, from anywhere

Stay in the loop on your loved one's wellbeing, even from across the country — with real alerts when it matters.

  • Check-in history and wellness trends
  • Instant SMS if a check-in is missed
  • Schedule volunteers on their behalf
  • View upcoming visits and events
  • Direct messaging with caregivers
🤝
For volunteers

Give your time where it counts

Browse nearby seniors who need companionship, errands, or tech help — claim a time slot and make a real difference.

  • Filter requests by type and distance
  • Claim visits and get reminders
  • Build a verified, rated profile
  • Background check verification badge
  • Tech tutoring match by skill set
Features

Everything built around
human connection

✅

Daily check-in system

Seniors tap in each morning. Missed check-ins automatically alert family by SMS — no setup required.

🗓️

Volunteer scheduling

Post a need, get matched with a nearby verified volunteer. Companionship, errands, or tech help.

💻

Tech tutor matching

Pair seniors with tech-savvy volunteers to learn video calling, email, and smartphone basics.

📅

Local events feed

Senior centers and community orgs post events — virtual and in-person — in one easy-to-browse feed.

🚨

Emergency SOS

One-tap emergency alert notifies the primary family contact instantly with the senior's location.

⭐

Volunteer ratings

Seniors and families rate each visit, building trust and accountability into every interaction.
